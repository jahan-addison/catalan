You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you give the operator permission to store, show, copy, and distribute it as part of running this site - on its own page, in the listing, as raw text, and in link previews.

Posts can't be changed or deleted through the site. Editing a post creates a new one, and the original stays. Unlisted posts are left out of the listing, but anyone with the link can read them, and links get shared, so never post anything secret.

## Bots and automation

Each new post needs a quick human check, by Cloudflare Turnstile. Reading posts can be automated within the rate limits, 60 requests a minute and 10 new posts a minute. Don't work around the limits or the human check.

## Privacy

There are no accounts, and this site sets no cookies of its own. It stores what you post: the title, author name, and text you enter, and the time. Your browser remembers the author name you last used, in its local storage, only on your device.

Your IP address isn't stored. It's used briefly to apply the rate limits, and sent to Cloudflare to verify the human check, under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The site's fonts may load from Adobe Fonts, under [Adobe's privacy policy](https://www.adobe.com/privacy/policy.html).
